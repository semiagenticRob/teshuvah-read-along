"I Woke Up!"

הַשְׁכָּמַת הַבֹּקֶר

Hashkamas HaBoker — the very first moments

Before you've said a word, before you've done a thing — you're already the recipient of a miracle. Feigenbaum's framing is radical in its simplicity: waking up isn't a given. It's a gift. And the first thing a gift demands is acknowledgment.

I Woke Up!

Modeh Aniמוֹדֶה אֲנִי

First words on waking — gratitude before you're fully awake

מוֹדֶה אֲנִי לְפָנֶֽיךָ מֶֽלֶךְ חַי וְקַיָּם שֶׁהֶחֱזַֽרְתָּ בִּי נִשְׁמָתִי בְּחֶמְלָה, רַבָּה אֱמוּנָתֶֽךָ.

Modeh ani l'fanecha melech chai v'kayam, she'hechezarta bi nishmati b'chemlah, rabbah emunatecha.

"Thank You, Hashem, for giving my neshamah back to me (which was a merciful thing to do since I have not yet lived up to my full potential, and therefore may not really deserve that); Your belief in me is very great! (You believe I am important; my struggles are important; and You believe I can climb up to the next rung — which is why You woke me up. Thank You!)"

"My Body Works!"

אֲשֶׁר יָצַר

Asher Yatzar — blessing recited after using the bathroom

Scientists spend careers on what this blessing covers in thirty seconds. Every organ, every passage, every cavity — functioning right now, without your permission or effort. Feigenbaum insists we feel this before we move on.

My Body Works!

Asher Yatzarאֲשֶׁר יָצַר

The body's miraculous, ordinary function — said every morning

בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְהֹוָה אֱלֹהֵֽינוּ מֶֽלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, אֲשֶׁר יָצַר אֶת הָאָדָם בְּחָכְמָה, וּבָרָא בוֹ נְקָבִים נְקָבִים, חֲלוּלִים חֲלוּלִים. גָּלוּי וְיָדוּעַ לִפְנֵי כִסֵּא כְבוֹדֶֽךָ, שֶׁאִם יִפָּתֵחַ אֶחָד מֵהֶם, אוֹ יִסָּתֵם אֶחָד מֵהֶם, אִי אֶפְשַׁר לְהִתְקַיֵּם וְלַעֲמוֹד לְפָנֶֽיךָ. בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְהֹוָה, רוֹפֵא כָל בָּשָׂר וּמַפְלִיא לַעֲשׂוֹת.

Baruch atah Adonai Eloheinu melech ha'olam, asher yatzar et ha'adam b'chochmah, u'vara vo n'kavim n'kavim, chalulim chalulim. Galui v'yadu'a lifnei chisei ch'vodecha, she'im yipate'ach echad meihem, o yisatem echad meihem, i efshar l'hitkayem v'la'amod l'fanecha. Baruch atah Adonai, rofei chol basar u'mafli la'asot.

"You have formed humans with great wisdom (as scientists are continually discovering) and created within us all sorts of openings and cavities. It is quite obvious that even if just one of them would be torn or blocked — it would be impossible for us to survive and stand in front of You (even for a second)! You, Hashem, are the Source of everything; You heal all living things and perform constant miracles."

"And I Have a Soul!"

אֱלֹהַי נְשָׁמָה

Elohai Neshamah — the soul's origin and destination

The body was remarkable. Now the soul. Feigenbaum's commentary on this blessing turns it into an autobiography: who put it there, how it's being kept alive, and what happens when it's taken back. All of it, addressed to you personally.

And I Have a Soul!

Elohai Neshamahאֱלֹהַי נְשָׁמָה

My God, the soul You gave me — pure

אֱלֹהַי נְשָׁמָה שֶׁנָּתַתָּ בִּי טְהוֹרָה הִיא. אַתָּה בְרָאתָהּ, אַתָּה יְצַרְתָּהּ, אַתָּה נְפַחְתָּהּ בִּי, וְאַתָּה מְשַׁמְּרָהּ בְּקִרְבִּי, וְאַתָּה עָתִיד לִיטְּלָהּ מִמֶּנִּי, וּלְהַחֲזִירָהּ בִּי לֶעָתִיד לָבוֹא. בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְהֹוָה, הַמַּחֲזִיר נְשָׁמוֹת לִפְגָרִים מֵתִים.

Elohai neshamah shenatata bi tehorah hi. Atah v'ratah, atah yetzartah, atah nefachtah bi, v'atah m'shamrah b'kirbi, v'atah atid lit'lah mimeni, u'lehachazirah bi le'atid lavo. Baruch atah Adonai, hamachazir neshamot lifgarim meitim.

"Hashem Who takes care of me — the neshamah that You personally placed in me is a pure neshamah! You formed it (uniquely for me); You breathed it into me, You make sure it stays in me (and keeps me alive); and I know that eventually You will take it back. (Therefore, I must take advantage of it while I can!) And I believe that You will return it to me at Techiyas Hameisim."

"I Have a Torah!"

בִּרְכוֹת הַתּוֹרָה

Birchot HaTorah — blessings before Torah study

From body to soul to purpose. The Torah blessings are where Feigenbaum gets pastoral — he knows many of us in this room feel ambivalent about the whole enterprise. He doesn't pretend that away. He just says: start anyway.

I Have a Torah!

Birchot HaTorahבִּרְכוֹת הַתּוֹרָה

Sanctifying Torah study — the day's first intellectual act

בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְהֹוָה אֱלֹהֵֽינוּ מֶֽלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, אֲשֶׁר קִדְּשָֽׁנוּ בְּמִצְוֹתָיו, וְצִוָּֽנוּ לַעֲסֹק בְּדִבְרֵי תוֹרָה.

Baruch atah Adonai Eloheinu melech ha'olam, asher kid'shanu b'mitzvotav, v'tzivanu la'asok b'divrei Torah.

"through Your mitzvos You elevated us; and You commanded us to be continually involved with the words of Torah. (Even though I do not always understand them or find them easy to apply.) But I know that Torah really IS sweet — and therefore I ask: Please, Hashem, make the words of Your Torah sweet (enjoyable and understandable) to us and to all Jews everywhere."

FAQ But I do NOT understand the Torah, and to be honest, I am NOT happy that I was stuck being part of the Chosen People! What's so great about it?

Famous question! The bedrock of our emunah is that Hashem spoke at Har Sinai and chose us for a mission. You have questions? Fine. It will take a lifetime to ask all the questions and get answers. But until that happens — get involved with the Torah, get with the program, do the best you can. A good understanding of Torah is given to those who practice it.

Instant Insight As much as we all wish that our daily experience of Torah and mitzvos be uplifting, meaningful, and exciting, the truth is that for many of us, at times in our lives it is not. But therefore, simply put, we must get with the program!
